# Getting Around

Movement in web2qml is a small spoken vocabulary. You don't navigate
menus — you say where you want to go, and the graph moves you there.

## The core words

- **"back"** — return to the node you came from.
- **"forward"** — retrace after going back.
- **"go home"** / **"show welcome"** — return to [[Welcome]] from anywhere.
- **"what can I do"** — open [[Capabilities]], the live list of what's
  available in your current context.

These work everywhere: while reading a page, in the terminal, on the web.
If you ever feel lost, **"go home"** always lands you back at the start.

## Three places you move between

1. **The graph** — where you are now. Nodes are pages, terminals, web
   views, and your saved work. Lines between them are links you can
   follow by voice or tap.

2. **The terminal** — a real shell. Say a command or type it. When a
   program like a debugger opens, the app notices and offers voice
   commands for it.

3. **The web** — say a site or a search out loud, or type a URL. Pages
   can be read aloud paragraph by paragraph.

## Saying vs tapping vs typing

Every one of these is interchangeable. The voice vocabulary is the
fastest to learn because the app surfaces it for you: any time you're
unsure, **"what can I do"** shows the words that work right here.

When you're comfortable, head to [[Capabilities]] to see the full set,
or go [[Welcome|back home]].
