# Welcome

You're looking at the **graph view** — the home of web2qml. Everything
here is a node you can move toward: pages, terminals, the web, your
voice profile. This welcome page is itself a node, and the lines leading
away from it go to the things you can do.

There's no menu to memorize. You explore by moving between nodes — by
voice, by tap, or by typing. Wherever you are, you can always come back
here by saying **"go home"** or **"show welcome."**

## Try saying it

If voice is on, the wake phrase is set in voice settings. Once you've
said it, try one of these out loud — each one carries you to the node
that explains it:

- Say **"what can I do"** to open [[Capabilities]]
- Say **"set up my voice"** to open [[Voice Setup]]
- Say **"how do I get around"** to open [[Getting Around]]

Prefer tapping? Each linked node above is also clickable. Prefer typing?
Type the same words in the bar at the bottom.

## The three things to know first

1. **It's a graph, not a stack of screens.** You move *between* places
   rather than drilling *into* them. Forward and back are always one
   word away — say **"back"** to return to the previous node, **"forward"**
   to retrace.

2. **Voice, tap, and type are interchangeable.** Anything you can say,
   you can tap or type. Pick whatever fits the moment.

3. **The app tells you what it can do.** You never have to guess — say
   **"what can I do"** from anywhere and you'll land on [[Capabilities]],
   the live list of everything available right now.

## Where to go next

The natural first step is [[Voice Setup]] — it builds your voice profile
so the app can recognize you, even with other people in the room. After
that, [[Getting Around]] shows the movement vocabulary.

When you're done here, say **"I'm ready"** or just start exploring. You
can reopen this page anytime with **"show welcome."**
