# Voice Setup

This is the voice-registration flow. Read the paragraph below
aloud at a normal speaking pace. The system will capture your
voice and use it to build a profile so it can recognize you
later, even when other people are in the room.

## Read this paragraph aloud

When you are ready, tap the voice button at the bottom right of
the screen and read the next paragraph clearly. You don't need
to speak loudly, but speak at the pace you'd use in a normal
conversation. The system will detect when you stop and capture
the audio automatically.

## The paragraph

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. She sells sea
shells by the sea shore. Peter Piper picked a peck of pickled
peppers. How much wood would a woodchuck chuck if a woodchuck
could chuck wood? These sentences cover most of the common
sounds in English, which helps the system learn how your voice
sounds across a range of vowels and consonants.

## What happens next

After you read, the system will analyze the audio and suggest a
color that represents your voice. You can accept the suggestion
or pick a different color. Then enter the name you want to be
known by, and tap Save.

The profile gets better over time as the system hears you in
normal use. You don't need to do this again unless you want to
add another person.
