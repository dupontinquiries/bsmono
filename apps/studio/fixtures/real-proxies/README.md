# real-proxies

Small downscaled proxies of the real footage (rights held by the project), for checking
tools on real pictures, where synthetic fixtures are too clean. 256x144, H.264, AAC mono.

- `allmylife-256.mp4` — the whole "All My Life" music video, 162.6 s, 30 fps (~1.7 MB).
  `allmylife-analysis.json` holds the earlier analysis: ESTIMATED cut times (not ground
  truth), looks per shot, and what held up (performer motion before cuts) and what did
  not (beat-locked cuts).
- `fragile-C0032-rooftop-wide-256.mp4` — "A Fragile Thing", the rooftop wide, 12 s from 40 s.
- `fragile-C0018-slate-256.mp4` — the slate take, its first 12 s.

The other rooftop angles and the recorder files live only in the original card archives.
