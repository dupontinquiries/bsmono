```
ACT 3: REVELATION & SACRIFICE
- Beat 3.1: Evacuation sequence
- Beat 3.2: Billy's commitment to remain
- Beat 3.3: Understanding Billy's nature shifts
- Beat 3.4: Expedition retreats, Billy visible in distance
```

### Step 2: Dialogue Draft

For each major scene, draft dialogue in the voices established in Trilogy 1:

- **Halyard Prime**: Youthful, determined, still learning leadership
- **Aiden**: Curious, grounded, bridge between human and Transformer
- **The Morpher**: Calibrated, precise, speaking from vast experience
- **Billy**: Intense, economic with words, operates on different priorities

### Step 3: Wire to Game

Once screenplay exists:

1. **Extract claims** from story beats
2. **Create findings** from dialogue, events, discoveries
3. **Update codex** with new details revealed
4. **Link to saga.rs** via stratum assignments
