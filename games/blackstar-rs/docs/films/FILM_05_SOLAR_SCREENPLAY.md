# TRANSFORMERS: SOLAR

## Film 5 of 9 — Trilogy 2 (Ascension Era), Position 2 of 3

**Status**: Reconciled replacement for the abandoned "Ember Deep" material.
Built from confirmed real canon (`docs/STORY_BIBLE.md`, `watch.rs`,
`codex.rs`) rather than the unmerged early-session plan. See
`CANON_DISCREPANCY_NOTE.md` for the reasoning behind the replacement.

**Premise**: The Coalition's fleet is operating past the shield boundary for
the first time (Film 4). The wonder doesn't last — the Hijacks are not
scouting anymore, they are converging, and Billy is already fighting them
alone at the edge of everything.

---

## ACT ONE — WHAT THE SHIELD WAS HIDING

### SCENE 1.1 — THE OUTER STATIONS

**Setting**: Weeks after the shield crossing. The *Ark's Wake* and a thin
line of Coalition outposts hold position past the boundary. The wonder of
Film 4 has curdled into something closer to vertigo — the crew now
understands how little of the cosmos they'd ever seen.

**Characters**: Halyard Prime, Aiden, the Morpher, Meridian, sensor staff

---

**MERIDIAN**
Sensor ghosts on three headings. Nothing that holds a shape long enough to
call a contact.

**RODIMUS PRIME**
Hijacks.

**THE MORPHER**
They stopped scouting eleven days ago. Now they hold position and wait,
which is a different kind of patience than reconnaissance. Something is
converging on this sector, and I do not yet know what.

**AIDEN**
Converging on what, exactly? There's nothing out here.

**THE MORPHER**
There is a position that matters. I have not told you which one, because I
was hoping I was wrong about needing to.

*A long beat. He isn't wrong.*

---

### SCENE 1.2 — FIRST CONTACT WITH BILLY

**Setting**: A drifting outpost at the sector's edge — scorched, alone,
clearly the site of an ongoing fight nobody back home knew was happening.

**Characters**: Halyard Prime, Aiden, Billy

---

*Wreckage. Hijack wreckage, specifically — dozens of hulls, cooling. In the
middle of it, a single figure: BILLY, a Fire Clone, standing rather than
resting, like standing is the only mode he has.*

**AIDEN**
*(quiet)*
Did he do all of this alone?

**RODIMUS PRIME**
*(over comms, to Billy)*
This is Halyard Prime, Coalition fleet. We're inbound to your position.

**BILLY**
*(doesn't turn around)*
You're thirty-one days late. Not that I asked.

**RODIMUS PRIME**
We didn't know this position existed until eleven days ago.

**BILLY**
Neither did the Hijacks, once. Now they won't stop finding it.

**AIDEN**
Why does it matter this much? What's out here?

**BILLY**
*(finally turns; there's nothing performative in it, just tired precision)*
Nothing you'd recognize as anything. That's the problem. They're not here
to conquer it. They're here to *understand* it, and they will keep sending
ships until they do, and they do not care how many of those ships stop
existing in the process.

**THE MORPHER (V.O.)**
*(over comms)*
Billy speaks plainly because it saves time. He is not being cold. He does
not have enough time left in most days to be anything else.

---

### SCENE 1.3 — THE SUN GUN, INTRODUCED

**Setting**: Billy's outpost, its one intact chamber. A weapon rig, clearly
built rather than issued — improvised into something formidable.

**Characters**: Billy, Aiden, Meridian

---

**AIDEN**
*(looking at the rig)*
What is that?

**BILLY**
The only thing out here that scares them.

**MERIDIAN**
It doesn't look like it's rated for what you're using it for.

**BILLY**
It isn't. That's why I have to aim it myself. The system can do the math.
It can't decide whether the shot is worth what it costs. That part's mine.

**AIDEN**
What does it cost?

**BILLY**
*(doesn't answer directly)*
Ask me after the next one.

---

## ACT TWO — THE POSITION CAN'T HOLD

### SCENE 2.1 — THE MORPHER'S PRIVATE ADMISSION

**Setting**: The *Ark's Wake*, a private channel. Halyard and the Morpher,
alone.

**Characters**: Halyard Prime, the Morpher

---

**RODIMUS PRIME**
Tell me the part you didn't say in front of Aiden.

**THE MORPHER**
The Watch shows me twelve forms clearly. I have mastered all twelve. There
is a thirteenth the Watch has only shown me once — by accident, in a battle
I do not remember clearly, for perhaps forty seconds. I woke three days
later with the Watch cold in my hand.

**RODIMUS PRIME**
And you think we're heading toward needing it deliberately.

**THE MORPHER**
I think Billy is buying us time to decide whether we're willing to ask him
to hold the line long enough for me to try.

**RODIMUS PRIME**
That's not a fair thing to ask of him.

**THE MORPHER**
No. It isn't. I'm asking anyway, because the alternative is worse, and
because I don't believe Billy would forgive me for *not* asking.

---

### SCENE 2.2 — THE HIJACK BUILDUP

**Setting**: Sensor bay. The scale of what's actually converging becomes
visible for the first time.

**Characters**: Halyard Prime, Meridian, Aiden, tactical staff

---

**MERIDIAN**
*(quiet, which is worse than urgent)*
Command. You need to see this.

*The display resolves. Not dozens of Hijack signatures. Hundreds.*

**AIDEN**
That's not a scouting fleet.

**MERIDIAN**
No. That's every ship they have in range, and they are not spreading out.
They are converging on Billy's position, specifically, like they've decided
whatever's there is worth the entire investment.

**RODIMUS PRIME**
*(to himself)*
They do not conquer. They gather data. Something out here is a big enough
question that they're willing to lose a fleet to answer it.

---

### SCENE 3.1 — THE LINE STARTS TO FAIL

**Setting**: Billy's position, under real siege now — the Coalition fleet
has arrived to reinforce, and it still isn't enough.

**Characters**: Billy, Halyard Prime, Aiden, Meridian, Coalition fleet

---

*Combat. Not choreographed heroism — attrition. Ships trading losses at a
rate the Coalition cannot sustain indefinitely.*

**BILLY**
*(over comms, steady even now)*
Halyard. However good your math is, it isn't good enough today.

**RODIMUS PRIME**
How long can you hold?

**BILLY**
Long enough to matter. Not long enough to win.

**AIDEN**
*(to Halyard, urgent)*
Tell him. Tell the Morpher.

**RODIMUS PRIME**
*(a long, heavy beat — this is the decision the whole film has been
building toward)*
*(into comms)*
Morpher. Do it.

---

### SCENE 3.2 — CLOSING IMAGE

**Setting**: The *Ark's Wake*'s bridge. The Morpher, alone in a sealed
chamber, prepares. Outside, the siege continues.

**Characters**: The Morpher, Halyard Prime (observing)

---

*The Morpher closes his eyes. The Watch, in his hand, is doing something
none of the room's instruments have a name for.*

**THE MORPHER**
*(quiet, almost to himself)*
Twelve forms, mapped and mastered. One more, that I do not master — that
masters me, for as long as I can survive holding it.

*(a beat)*

I hope this works. I hope, more than that, that it is worth what it will
cost.

*The chamber floods with light that has no discernible source. Cut to
black — the transformation itself is Film 6's cold open, not this film's
closing shot.*

**END OF FILM 5**
