# TRANSFORMERS: PHOTON

## Film 6 of 9 — Trilogy 2 (Ascension Era), Position 3 of 3

**Status**: Reconciled replacement for the abandoned "Convergence" material.
The climax uses the improved battle sequence (gravity-warp reinforcement,
Billy and the Sun Gun) confirmed as the intended final battle, drawn from
the reference archive and adapted to fit real canon rather than the
abandoned Matter Transformation Planet plot. See
`CANON_DISCREPANCY_NOTE.md` and `BLACKSTAR_AND_BLACK_ARCHIVES_RESEWN.md`.

**Premise**: The Morpher reaches for Photon Man deliberately, for the first
time in the Watch's long history. What answers isn't rescue arriving on
schedule — it's a species that has never had to retreat from anything
recognizing, for the first time, something categorically beyond it.

---

## ACT ONE — THE COST OF THE CHOICE

### SCENE 1.1 — COLD OPEN: THE ROOM GOES WHITE

**Setting**: Continuous from Film 5's final image. The sealed chamber.

**Characters**: The Morpher, Rodimus Prime (observing, helpless)

---

*Light with no source floods the frame. Not an explosion — an emergence.
When it clears, the Morpher is standing, and everyone in the room
understands without being told that whatever is standing there is not,
right now, entirely him.*

**RODIMUS PRIME**
*(quiet, to the room)*
Hold position. Nobody touches the door until he tells us to.

---

### SCENE 1.2 — THE SIEGE, WORSENING

**Setting**: Billy's position. The line is close to breaking outright.

**Characters**: Billy, Aiden, Meridian, Coalition fleet remnants

---

**MERIDIAN**
Losses at forty percent and climbing. Command, I need a decision on
withdrawal.

**BILLY**
*(over comms, not panicked, just accounting)*
If you withdraw, this position is gone in under ten minutes.

**AIDEN**
So we don't withdraw.

**BILLY**
So somebody upstairs needs to finish whatever they're doing very soon.

## ACT TWO — THE FIRST JUMP

### SCENE 2.1 — SILENCE, THEN GRAVITY

**Setting**: Open space near the siege line. A normal starfield — briefly.

**Characters**: none speaking; pure sequence

---

*Almost no sound design. A held, uncomfortable quiet.*

*Then: not a portal. Gravity itself bends — the stars behind the distortion
visibly stretch, smear, snap back. A fleet resolves out of the distortion.
Then another. Then another.*

*On the bridge, nobody speaks for a beat too long.*

**MERIDIAN**
*(finally)*
Those aren't our ships. I mean — they are. I don't know how they got here
that fast.

**RODIMUS PRIME**
*(realizing)*
They didn't approach. They warped the space between here and the rest of
the Coalition's reserve, and repositioned the whole formation at once.

---

### SCENE 2.2 — INSIDE THE HIJACK FORMATIONS

**Setting**: The siege, reversed in an instant.

**Characters**: Billy, Meridian, Hijack fleet (non-speaking, procedural)

---

*The reinforcing fleet isn't arriving at the perimeter. It's arriving
*inside* the Hijack formations — appearing in the gaps between their own
ships, where there is no room to maneuver away from it.*

**BILLY**
*(a rare note of something like relief)*
Well. That's new.

**MERIDIAN**
They're surrounded. Command, the Hijacks are surrounded.

---

### SCENE 2.3 — "YOU HAVE ONE SHOT"

**Setting**: Billy's position, the Sun Gun rig.

**Characters**: Billy, Meridian

---

**MERIDIAN**
Billy. This is it. You have one shot before their formation adapts.

**BILLY**
I know.

*He doesn't rush. The Sun Gun's charge climbs, and he watches it climb with
something closer to calm than urgency.*

**BILLY**
*(quiet)*
Let's go.

*FIRE.*

---

### SCENE 2.4 — THE ARTIFICIAL SUNRISE

**Setting**: The battle space, transformed by the shot itself.

**Characters**: everyone, watching

---

*Not an explosion. Not a beam in the conventional sense. The Sun Gun's
discharge creates a controlled solar event across the engagement zone — for
several seconds, the battlefield is lit as if a sun has risen inside it.*

*Every ship, every gravity-warp distortion, every hull is silhouetted at
once. For the first time, everyone present — on both sides — sees the true
scale of what's actually happening.*

*The light collapses. Silence. Billy is still standing. The rig is
smoking.*

**MERIDIAN**
*(into comms, barely a whisper)*
Did we win?

**BILLY**
*(looking out at the wreckage)*
Ask me in a minute.

---

## ACT THREE — WHAT RETREAT LOOKS LIKE WHEN NOTHING HAS EVER MADE THEM DO IT BEFORE

### SCENE 3.1 — THE HIJACKS WITHDRAW

**Setting**: The battle space, aftermath.

**Characters**: Rodimus Prime, Aiden, the Morpher (recovering), Meridian

---

*The surviving Hijack ships do not regroup. They do not counterattack. They
leave — not tactically, not to preserve resources for a later engagement.
They leave the way something leaves when it has just recognized a category
of threat it has no model for.*

**THE MORPHER**
*(weakened, being supported, but present)*
They do not conquer. They gather data. What just happened is data they did
not expect to gather.

**AIDEN**
Is that good?

**THE MORPHER**
It answers one question and opens ten more, which is generally what
progress looks like out here.

---

### SCENE 3.2 — THE COST, PAID

**Setting**: Medical bay. The Morpher, three days into what everyone
understands will be a three-day recovery.

**Characters**: Rodimus Prime, Aiden, Billy

---

**RODIMUS PRIME**
*(quiet, at the Morpher's side)*
You were right. It was worth it.

**AIDEN**
*(to Billy, who's leaning in the doorway, uncharacteristically still)*
You okay?

**BILLY**
*(a long beat — this is as close as he gets to processing anything aloud)*
I've been alone out here a long time. I didn't know how much I was
expecting to still be alone after today.

*He doesn't finish the thought. He doesn't need to.*

---

### SCENE 3.3 — CLOSING IMAGE

**Setting**: The siege line, now quiet. Wreckage — theirs and the Hijacks'
both — drifting in the light of a sun that, for a few seconds today,
briefly had company.

**Characters**: Rodimus Prime (V.O.)

---

**RODIMUS PRIME (V.O.)**
We came out here thinking we were at war. What we found out today is that
we were being studied — and that whatever's out here studying us just
learned something it didn't expect to find.

*(a beat)*

I don't know yet whether that makes things safer or considerably less
safe. I know it makes them different. Trilogy Two ends here — not with a
victory. With a first real look at how large the actual question is.

**END OF FILM 6 — END OF TRILOGY TWO**
