EXPANSION ACHIEVEMENTS
=====================
Content:
  • Cards: 22 → 30 (8 new: Raven Crows, Ink Golem, Tidal Wave, Piper,
    Void Critter, Frost Mage, Iron Smith, Shadow Cat)
  • Arenas: 6 → 9 (3 new: Sunken Library, Ice Cavern, Corrupted Forest)
  • Spells: 0 → 5 (Fire Blast, Heal, Petrify, Surge, Meteor)

Audio & Voice:
  • Voice system: TTS (pyttsx3) with async playback
  • SFX system: Procedural tone generation (600Hz whoosh, 1200Hz spell, etc.)
  • Mute toggle and graceful fallback

Multiplayer:
  • LAN server (UDP, port 7777)
  • LAN client connection framework
  • 2v2 mode flag and shared team elixir concept
  • JSON message protocol designed

Performance:
  • Object pooling design sketched
  • Spatial hashing for O(1) unit queries
  • Sprite batching planned
  • Cache-based SFX to avoid GC stalls

Graphics:
  • Arena-specific color palettes
  • Weather effects per arena
  • Spell glow colors defined
  • Hazard visual systems
