## Grounding correction (worth flagging)
The brief lists `sim::water_physics` **or** `sim::buoyancy`. In this tree
`sim::water_physics` is the shallow-water **heightfield hydrology** step
(flow/evaporation/soil-moisture; its `WaterParams` is unrelated). **Body** water-
physics belongs in `buoyancy` (it already owns submerged-fraction + buoyant accel +
fluid drag + the float integrator), so the new core went there. No collision with
`water_physics::WaterParams` (distinct `WaterPhysicsParams`).

## The bonus — "put their head above water"
A body settles under natural buoyancy at `submerged = body_density/fluid_density`,
so a near-water-density swimmer (ρ≈1000–1010) bobs *at* the surface and a denser one
sinks — neither reliably keeps the **head** out. `surface_accel` adds an **upward
restoring assist** while the player is actively surfacing/treading (`surfacing=true`):
it lifts the body toward the waterline where `submerged == surface_submersion`
(default `0.9` → the top 10 %, the head, is above water), capped at `3 g` so the
swimmer rises to the surface but never launches out. Stop treading (`surfacing=false`)
and natural buoyancy takes over — a denser body's head goes back under.

Proof (`head_above_water_proof.png`, generated by the **real** `swim_vertical_step`):
a ρ=1005 body starting 2 m under rises to **+0.15 m head-above-water** at submerged
≈0.915 (≈ the 0.9 target, small damped overshoot) when treading; the same body sinks
when idle.

## Integration (propose — do NOT wire; SHARED_RULES §1/§2)
In `physics.rs::step_body` (the body integrator; `constants::GRAVITY = 28.0`,
note the **game-unit** gravity — pass it straight in, the surface assist is
gravity-relative so it scales correctly; `swim_accel`/`max_swim_speed` are absolute
m/s and already sane):

1. Detect immersion: `in_water01` from the body AABB vs the local water surface
   (the engine already has an in-water notion — `motion_factor_slows_body_in_water`).
   Compute `submerged = buoyancy::submerged_fraction(min_y, max_y, water_level)`.
2. **Horizontal/general swim:** when `in_water01 > 0`, replace land accel with
   `vel = buoyancy::swim_step(vel, input_dir, in_water01, dt, &params)` and add
   `buoyancy::flow_push(flow_at_body, in_water01, dt)` for currents (flow vector from
   `water_field`/flow data on the bin side).
3. **Vertical + head-above-water:** drive the vertical channel with
   `(y, vy) = buoyancy::swim_vertical_step(y, vy, min_y, max_y, water_level, surfacing,
   body_density, fluid_density, GRAVITY, dt, &params)`.
   - `surfacing` = the player holding swim-up / jump / a "tread" toggle while in water
     (`apply_input`@302 already reads jump). Map jump-held-in-water → `surfacing=true`.
   - `body_density` per entity (player ≈ 1010, log ≈ 500, stone ≈ 2600); `fluid_density`
     = water ≈ 1000.
4. `head_above_water(max_y, water_level)` is the predicate the camera/breath/UI uses
   (e.g. stop the drowning timer, switch to the above-water camera) once the head clears.

`WaterPhysicsParams::default()` is tuned for a human/rider swimmer; expose it per-entity
if creatures need different handling. Couple to T-water-visual only via docs (different
files: physics vs visual).
