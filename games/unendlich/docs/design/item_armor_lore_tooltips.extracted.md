Tooltip Format Specification
Every item in the game uses a consistent tooltip structure. Lore text appears in italics beneath the mechanical effect lines, attributed to a source within the world. Attributions use one of six source types:

Attribution type
Format and usage

Teaching lines from bending dojo boards. Attributed to the dojo or a named master. Used for bending instruments and technique-related items.
Ancient Shrine text
Fragmentary, partially untranslated inscriptions from pre-civilisation shrine builders. Used for Ancient items. Format: '[partial transcription] — Shrine [location], East Wall' or similar.
Ryujin Triad record

Recovered fragment
Undated or anonymous text recovered from ruins or wreckage. Used for items with unclear origin. Format: 'Recovered fragment, [site name].'



  ⚡  Bending Instruments — Focus Stones, Catalysts, Reagents

Bending Instruments
Focus stones are the primary bending instruments — held in hand or worn at the chest, they modify the player's effective environmental property thresholds. A focus stone does not give the player new abilities; it makes existing techniques available in a wider range of conditions, or amplifies their effect within current conditions. Catalysts are secondary instruments that modify specific technique properties without changing property thresholds.
