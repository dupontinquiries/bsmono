Screen Layout — Canonical Three-Cluster Arrangement
The reference image (Fire / Forge-Hardened) establishes the canonical layout that all six discipline panels follow. Key differences from the single-constellation Water panel:

Element
Description
Super diamond icon
A standalone large diamond-frame icon placed left of the three clusters, outside all rings. The super ability symbol is displayed at 4× normal node scale. Gold-framed, element-accent filled, the player's identity technique. Clicking navigates to the super ability's dedicated detail view.
Upper cluster
4–5 nodes in a cross/diamond arrangement. Modifier and passive techniques. These nodes buff the core active techniques — they do not produce effects on their own. Smaller ring diameter. Virtual depth 0.8 (slightly soft in DOF).
Middle cluster
The largest cluster: 4 cardinal nodes on the main ring plus 2–4 outer modifier nodes. Core active techniques — the ones the player binds and uses. Virtual depth 0.6 (sharpest of the three clusters). This is the cluster the eye reads first.
Lower-left cluster
3 nodes in a triangular arrangement. Foundation passives — always-on properties that define the archetype. Cannot be unequipped. Lowest position on screen. Virtual depth 1.2 (softest).
Character
Right-anchored, filling the right 40% of the screen. Focal plane — sharpest element. Rendered at ultra quality with full tessellation, SSS, cloth simulation. The weapon or held instrument is in the foreground, element-active (glowing, animated).
Path code name
Bottom-right, below the character. Larger typography than other labels. This is the sub-discipline path name — equivalent to 'CODE OF THE FIRE-FORGED' in the reference image. Spaced lettering, element accent colour.
Sub-discipline label
Top-left, below the discipline name. Two lines: discipline name (e.g., 'FORGE BREAKER') and archetype tier (e.g., 'HEAVY BENDER ARCHETYPE').

Panel 2 — Air Bending: Wind Dancer

The Atmospheric Current path specialises in sustained flight, pressure differential attacks, and wind-assisted mobility. The character is lean and unarmoured by comparison, holding balance rather than weight.

Panel 3 — Earth Bending: Stone Warden

The Deep Fault path specialises in raw geological power — rising columns, impenetrable walls, and the seismic pulse that communicates with the earth itself. The character is planted, heavy, immovable.

Panel 4 — Information Bending: Echo Reader
The Information bending panel is the most visually alien — it looks least like the others. The Resonant Bender archetype: reconnaissance, acoustic archaeology, light manipulation. The Temporal Path specialises in reading echoes, storing spatial resonance, and disorienting opponents by making the light lie about where things are. The character is the smallest visual presence of all six panels — intentionally. Information benders are defined by what they perceive, not what they project.

  ◆  ECHO READER
  RESONANT BENDER ARCHETYPE    ·    CODE OF THE REMEMBERED LIGHT

  SUPER TECHNIQUE — STANDALONE DIAMOND ICON
  ◈  Temporal Cascade  —  Releases all stored Temporal Echo data simultaneously as an acoustic-visual burst. All creatures within 30 blocks experience a 4-second disorientation — their vision shows the acoustic ghost of the location 3 minutes ago superimposed on the present. Targets with low-certainty ghost records in their mob brain are particularly susceptible (the cascade feeds their object permanence system false certainty). Chi cost: 80. Recharges on: each successful Temporal Echo Read that yields new information.
  The diamond super icon is displayed at 4× node scale, left of centre, outside all cluster rings. It is the focal element of the left panel — the player's identity technique. Not part of any cluster. Always unlocked.

  ELEMENTAL VOID — ENVIRONMENT
  The void for Information bending does not represent a physical location — it represents a moment of interference. Two overlapping circular wavefronts fill the frame, their intersections producing the characteristic bright-dark interference pattern from the Information void shader. The overall field is dark — very dark, near-black base — with bright teal-white caustic lines tracing the interference peaks. At the top of the frame: a slight prismatic split where the wavefronts create chromatic dispersion, RGB channels shifting apart by 4–6px. Tiny, scattered pinpoint light sources throughout (not particles — they are fixed, like stars, suggesting this is a representation of information itself rather than any physical space). The full moon is not present here — instead, a faint perfect circle of teal light in the upper third marks the source of one wavefront. Another fainter circle, offset, marks the second. The character stands at their intersection.
  LUT: Prismatic clinical. Neutral midtones, cyan-green push. Split highlights warm/cool. Medium contrast. No vignette (the open void of information extends to all edges).. DOF: Character right-anchor but smaller visual presence (30% of screen width vs 40% for Fire). Focal plane at the Scatter Lens (right hand). f/1.4 — shallowest DOF of all six: only the lens and the hand holding it are fully sharp. Everything else, including most of the character's own body, is slightly soft. This is intentional — an Information bender exists slightly outside the frame of reality. Void wavefront pattern: 22px blur. Clusters: 6–12px.

  CHARACTER — RIGHT-ANCHORED
  The lightest armour of all six panels — the Scatter Mantle set (from item lore). The cloak is nearly translucent at the edges: the alpha fade on the hem is visible (the Scatter Mantle's prismatic shell enchantment creates soft optical edges where the fabric meets air). Under the cloak: close-fitted dark material with a faint bioluminescent thread woven through it — visible only at close zoom, a property of Information bending cloth (cave glider membrane content in the weave). The character holds the Scatter Lens focus stone at eye level in the right hand — the lens faces the camera, and through it the background interference pattern is seen slightly refracted (the lens distorts the void behind it). Left hand: palm open, fingers spread in a sustained Scatter Field gesture. Posture: very still. Unlike the Warden's planted stillness, this is a focused stillness — every part of the body deliberately placed. SSS: the teal bioluminescent thread in the cloth produces very faint green-teal sub-scatter at the wrist and inner arm.
