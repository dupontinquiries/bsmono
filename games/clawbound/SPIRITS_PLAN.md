Rather than simply unlocking powers, a Clawbound cat might earn the favor of these First Spirits through quests, visions, or sacred trials. Their relics are not purchased like MOBA items but discovered in forgotten dens, won through seasonal ceremonies, or inherited as clan heirlooms, making each ancestor feel like part of the world's mythology rather than just a source of combat abilities.
