#     mesh (a ripped game asset); others are trademark or trade dress (a model
#     of a real car or aircraft), which is a different question with different
#     answers depending on use. They are grouped so you can take the sections
#     separately. I am not a lawyer and this list is a starting point for your
#     review, not a ruling.
